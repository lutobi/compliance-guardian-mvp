# Project Execution Plan (PEP) — TiN STS04–STS06 Connections

- Project: Terminal Investment Namibia (TiN) — STS cranes electrical connection (MV) and communications (FO)
- Version: 0.1 (draft)
- Date: 2025-08-26
- Prepared by: Selex Engineering

## 1. Purpose
Provide a clear plan to execute the electrical and communications scope safely, on time, and to specification, with evidence-led quality and audit-ready documentation.

## 2. Scope Overview
- Electrical (MV): STS04 migration to CTP5; new JB+Drum at CTP6 for STS05/STS06; protection settings coordination; IR/VLF testing; legalisation dossier.
- Communications (FO/OT): Cargo Building rack terminations; FO star to CTP5/CTP6; closures and LC/UPC panels; OT segmentation and acceptance (OTDR/IL/Polarity).

## 3. Objectives
- Safety first: zero-energized work; PTW/LOTO; confined space/lifting controls.
- Compliance: align to RFP V3.1 and applicable IEC/TIA standards.
- Quality: 100% ITP adherence; traceable evidence (photos, traces, torque logs, settings sign-off).
- Handover-ready: complete as-built and legalisation dossier with signed ATPs.

## 4. Deliverables (Summary)
- Design package: SLD, GA for CTP5/CTP6 pits, cable schedules, splice maps.
- Materials & COC: MV cables/kits; IP68 closures; LC/UPC panels; labels; vendor certificates.
- Installation works: MV JB+Drum assemblies; FO pulls/splices/terminations; rack works.
- Testing: IR/VLF (MV); OTDR/IL/polarity (FO); protection setting verification.
- Documentation: ITP/ITRs, redlines to as-built DWG/PDF, labeling schedules, O&M manual, training records, legalisation dossier.

## 5. Milestones & Gantt
See `deliverables/pep/gantt.html` for a visual Gantt. Key milestones:
- M1: Kick-off & Surveys complete
- M2: IDC/IDR approved for IFC
- M3: Long-lead materials received
- M4: CTP5/CTP6 installations complete
- M5: Testing & Commissioning passed (Electrical + Communications)
- M6: Handover & Legalisation dossier submitted

## 6. Organisation & Responsibilities (RACI summary)
- Project Manager (PM): plan, report, risk/change control — A/R
- Electrical Lead: MV design, installation, testing — R
- Comms/OT Lead: FO design, splicing, OT acceptance — R
- HSE Lead: RAMS, PTW/LOTO, audits — R
- QA/QC Lead: ITP/ITR, inspections, evidence management — R
- Client Rep (TiN): reviews/approvals, witness points — A/C

## 7. Quality Plan & ITP
- ITP stages mapped to ITRs (Hold/Witness): ITR-10/20/30/40/50.
- Evidence capture: photo logs; IR/VLF traces; OTDR/IL/Polarity; torque records; instrument calibration certificates.
- Non-conformance control: NCR logging, root cause, corrective/preventive actions.

## 8. HSE & RAMS
- Method statements + task-specific RAMS.
- PTW/LOTO workflow; confined-space and lifting plans.
- Toolbox talks; stop-work authority; near-miss and incident reporting.

## 9. Method Statements (Summary)
- Electrical: isolation and verification; pit hardware prefabrication; terminations; earthing; protection settings and verification.
- Comms: FO pull; splice at closures; panel terminations; cleaning and inspection (IEC/TIA); rack dressing to standard.

## 10. Risk Register (initial)
- R1: Late material delivery — Mitigation: early PO, expediting, alternates.
- R2: Outage window constraints — Mitigation: flexible shifts; detailed cutover plan; pre-fabrication.
- R3: Underground congestion — Mitigation: surveys, trial holes, reroute options.
- R4: Weather delays — Mitigation: weather windows, temp covers, float.
- R5: Test failures — Mitigation: pre-tests, mockups, calibrated gear, rework windows.

## 11. Communications & Reporting
- Weekly progress meetings; monthly steering.
- Daily site diary with progress photos.
- KPIs: HSE, schedule, quality (ITP adherence), risks, change log.

## 12. Change Control
- Baseline scope/schedule; managed change requests with impact assessment.
- No scope change without written approval.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria
- Electrical: IR, VLF, earthing continuity, protection settings verified and signed.
- Comms: OTDR bi-directional at 1310/1550 nm; insertion loss; polarity pass; labeling audit passed.

## 14. Documentation & Handover
- As-built DWG/PDF; labeling schedules; test packs; O&M; training; legalisation dossier.

## 15. Assumptions & Dependencies
- Employer-supplied switchgear per V3.1; access to outage windows; timely design approvals.
