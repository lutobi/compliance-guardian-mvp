General Arrangement — MV Cable Drum (single cable)

Design basis: One MV cable per drum. Assumed cable OD ≈ 65 mm (Prysmian table ~3×120 mm²). Sizing rules: Barrel Ø ≥ 18×OD (≥ 1170 mm, use 1200 mm nominal). Flange Ø ≥ Barrel + 6×OD (≥ 1560 mm, use 1600 mm nominal). Min edge clearance from cable pack to flange ≥ 2×OD (≥ 130 mm). Traverse pitch ≈ 1.05×OD (≈ 68 mm). Bore sized for spindle/jacks.
Notes: Confirm final OD from selected MV cable datasheet; adjust barrel/flange accordingly. For very long lengths, increase width and check total mass vs. lifting equipment. Maintain OEM reeling instructions and min. free radius in payout path.
