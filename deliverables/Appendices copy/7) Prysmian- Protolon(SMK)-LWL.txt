                           PROTOLON(SMK)-LWL (N)TSKCGEWOEU 6/10 kV:
                            medium voltage reeling cable with fibre-optics




Application
Flexible medium voltage reeling cable with integrated fibre-optics for the combined transmission of energy and data, for application under high
or extreme mechanical stresses, e.g. high travel speeds, dynamic tensile loads, multiple changes of direction into different planes, churning on
running over rollers and torsional stresses.
Mainly for mobile equipment, e.g. fast-moving container cranes and large moving equipment.
