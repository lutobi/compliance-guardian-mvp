### Detailed Design Notes
- Enclosure & ingress: SS316/SS304 C5M, IP67/IK10, padlockable; neoprene gaskets; all unused entries sealed with listed blanking plugs.
- Terminations: heat-shrink/cold-applied stress-control kits per IEC 60502-2; follow manufacturer strip templates; record crimp die/press cycles.
- Earthing: tinned copper earth bar with labeled test link; 25–35 mm² bonding to grid; verify continuity < 1 Ω and log.
- Glanding & strain relief: double-compression glands with armor continuity as applicable; support saddles within 150 mm of entry; maintain min bend radius per OEM.
- Venting/condensation: IP-rated filtered breathers at low differential pressure; desiccant packs where humidity risk; drain path clear.
- Labeling & traceability: UV-stable engraved phenolic plates; phase ID sleeves; torque labels listing Nm values and date/tech ID; asset tag/QR linked to CMMS.
- Corrosion & marine measures: isolating washers, anti-galvanic paste on dissimilar metals; fasteners A4-80; external fittings coated or anodized.
- Clearances & segregation: maintain creepage/clearance per voltage class; segregate IN/OUT cores with barriers; shrouds/boots on live parts.
- Maintenance access: 20% spare terminal capacity where practical; clearance for torque tool; hinged gland plate optional for ease of service.

### JB Close-Up Checklist (Before Sealing Enclosure)
- Terminations dressed; required creepage/clearance maintained; no exposed conductive burrs.
- Stress-control components seated per kit template; semicon cleaned; anti-tracking sleeves (where specified) installed.
- Lugs crimped with calibrated dies; record die code, number of compressions, tool ID, last calibration date.
- Terminal torques applied per table; apply torque verification marks; record in torque log.
- Armor/braid bonding continuity verified (where applicable); gland locknuts secured; earthing to Cu bar with labeled test link.
- Phase identification sleeves and permanent engraved labels applied; cable IDs match schedule.
- Breather orientation and drain path confirmed; desiccant pack installed/replaced; gasket intact, clean, continuous.
- Photo evidence captured (overall layout, each termination, earth link, nameplate/labels); ITR cross-reference added.
- Witness/Hold point signed as per ITP (pre close-up); install torque label/plate inside door.

### Drum Handling, Storage & Deployment
- Storage: keep drums under cover on level surface with chocks; protect from direct sun/UV and standing water; maintain end caps/seals.
- Handling: lift with spreader or forklift under flanges only; never insert tines through drum core; use spindle stands with controlled brake.
- Pulling: use capstan or winch with in-line dynamometer; comply with max pulling tension and sidewall pressure per OEM; record start/peak/end tensions.
- Bend radius: maintain ≥ 12×D (or OEM spec) during handling and installation; deploy corner rollers and rub blocks at changes in direction.
- Lubrication: apply approved cable lubricant uniformly; avoid over-application that could contaminate terminations.
- Twist control: keep flanges vertical; pay-off in the direction of arrow; avoid reverse bends and crossovers; no dragging on ground.
- Post-pull: seal ends immediately; allow relaxation dwell time before cutting/terminating; update Cable Pull Log (route, length, tensions, weather).

## Method Statement
- Pre-conditions and permits
  Prior to any site activity, we will confirm the final tie-in arrangement with TiN, issue IFC drawings and ITPs, and obtain all permits to work (PTW) and LOTO authorizations. A task-specific RAMS briefing will be held with the crew, including lifting plans for drums and confined-space controls for pit work. Materials will be inspected against certificates of conformity and staging areas will be set up with weather protection.

- STS04 migration (CTP6 → CTP5)
  1) Isolation and LOTO: Coordinate outage window with TiN; identify isolation points; apply locks/tags with unique IDs; prove dead with a calibrated tester (test before-use and after); apply portable earths (rated for prospective fault current) where required; record in permit log.
  2) Route verification: rod-and-rope CTP6→CTP5; mandrel sized per cable OD + margin; CCTV if resistance; dewater pits using filtered pumps; confirm confined-space controls; document obstructions and agree minor civil remediation if required; update survey sketch.
  3) Install CTP5 JB & drum: set base; mount SS (C5M) enclosure with isolating washers; fit earth bar/test link; prepare gland plates; seal unused entries; position and secure drum assembly; provide slack loops and mechanical protection at pit exit; maintain access clearances.
  4) Cable handling and pull: position donor/receiving stations with rollers, bell mouths, rub blocks; use capstan winch with in-line dynamometer; approved lubricant; rollers at bends; maintain min bend radius/sidewall pressure per OEM; record tension and pull length in the pull log; communication between pull teams via radios.
  5) Terminations at CTP5: measure/cut; strip per template; abrade/clean semicon; install stress-control/insulation tubes and lugs; crimp with calibrated die set; heat-shrink/gel per sequence; fit boots/sealing mastic/anti-tracking sleeves; dress/torque to spec; record in torque log; apply phase ID and permanent labels; photograph steps for QA.
  6) Disconnection at CTP6: safely disconnect STS04 feeders; cap/insulate; update labels to reflect new routing; remove temporary earths after tests; tidy and secure enclosure; update as-built notes.

- STS05/STS06 new feeders at CTP6
  1) Pit preparation: clean/inspect CTP6 pits; mount two JB enclosures on corrosion-resistant fixings; verify level and door swing clearance; install filtered breathers and gland plates; confirm IP integrity.
  2) Earthing: bond each JB to the grid with tinned copper; labeled test links; verify continuity to grid (< 1 Ω target) and document readings; check sheath bonding scheme.
  3) Drums and feeders: position drums with spindle stands/brake control; pull feeders from mobile substation to CTP6; maintain bend radius and use rollers at direction changes; protect pit entries with grommets/bushings; seal with rated compounds; protect against chafe.
  4) Terminations and labeling: complete stress-control terminations; torque check; apply phase/feeder labels; fit engraved plates per TiN schema; dress conductors to avoid mechanical stress; photo record each termination.
  5) Protection settings: receive TiN-approved setting sheet; apply/verify settings on source protection; document all parameters; prepare coordination plots; store settings files securely with versioning.

- Inspections and hold points
  Hold/witness points will be observed at isolation verification, completed terminations (before enclosure close-up), and pre-energization test review. TiN representatives will be invited to witness per ITP. Photo evidence and ITR references will be embedded; punch items, if any, will be closed before energization and documented in the punch list register.

## Testing & Commissioning
- Visual and mechanical checks
  Verify enclosure IP integrity, gland seals, torque on terminals, and completeness of labels/marking. Confirm earthing connections and test link status. Review torque and pull logs for completeness.

- Insulation resistance (IR) and continuity
  Perform IR per conductor and conductor-to-earth using a calibrated megohmmeter at the correct test voltage for the circuit rating (e.g., 5 kV DC for 6–11 kV class, 10 kV DC for higher ratings as applicable). Record temperature-corrected values and compare to manufacturer and project thresholds. Verify conductor continuity and phase identification end-to-end; check R1/R2 as applicable.

- VLF/withstand testing
  Conduct Very-Low-Frequency withstand at 0.1 Hz at the specified kV level (e.g., 2×U0) for the prescribed duration (typically 30 minutes) per IEC/IEEE guidance. Monitor for instability or partial discharge indications if instrumented. Acceptance: no breakdown, test completes at setpoint with stable current profile.

- Earthing tests
  Measure pit-to-grid continuity (< 1 Ω target) and, where required, perform earth resistance tests using clamp or 3-point methods. Verify sheath bonding continuity per design.

- Protection verification
  Secondary injection: Verify relay pickup, time-current characteristics, instantaneous elements, and lockout logic against the TiN setting sheet. Check CT polarity and ratio. Where feasible, perform end-to-end functional trip checks to confirm breaker operation and interlocks.

- Energization procedure
  Hold a pre-energization review. Remove temporary earths, restore enclosures, and clear the work area. Stage energization: first no-load verification (phase rotation, voltage presence), then incremental load. Perform a thermal scan after 2–4 hours of load to identify hotspots; rectify and re-scan if anomalies are detected. Compile the test dossier with signed sheets and as-built redlines.

## Engineering & Design Approach — Electrical (MV)
Our electrical design begins with a clear definition of operational scenarios and design criteria agreed with TiN. We derive design current from ZPMC OEM data and confirm upstream fault levels and protection philosophy. Cable sizing considers ampacity, voltage drop, short-circuit thermal withstand, and installation conditions. Protection settings are coordinated across source breakers, feeders and any intermediate relays; outputs include a formal setting sheet, coordination curves, and remarks on selectivity and clearing times. Earthing is designed to IEC 61936-1 with explicit targets for step/touch voltage and pit-to-grid continuity; sheath bonding is specified to avoid circulating currents while maintaining fault return paths. Marine exposure is addressed through C5M-grade stainless hardware, IP67 enclosures, sealed glands, and protected routing. All terminations are specified with manufacturer torque values and permanent labeling per TiN schema, with torque logs captured in the ITP.

### Step-by-Step Engineering Workflow
1) FEED data collection: loads, fault level, tie-in strategy; routes survey and duct occupancy; preliminary risk log.
2) Calculations: cable sizing/VD; short-circuit thermal checks; earthing targets; document assumptions and margins.
3) Protection coordination: settings sheet; curves; selectivity notes; CT/VT ratio checks; relay model/firmware compatibility.
4) Drawings: SLDs; pit GA; term schedules; labeling schema; torque tables; review checklists.
5) Reviews: IDC/IDR with TiN; actions closed; IFC issuance; version control per doc plan.
6) Preconstruction brief: method statements; ITP checkpoints; hold/witness invites; pre-mob risk review.

## Procurement & Logistics Plan

## Engineering Calculations & Acceptance Criteria (Electrical)
### MV Cable Sizing & Volt Drop (example criteria)
- Design current Ib from ZPMC load; select cable with Iz ≥ Ib·k (derating for ambient, grouping).
- Short-circuit check: I²t withstand vs prospective Ik''; verify thermal/mechanical withstand per IEC 60949.
- Volt drop: ΔV = I·(R·cosφ + X·sinφ)·L. Acceptance: ≤ 5% at full load feeder end (project criterion), ≤ 3% typical.
- Termination torque per manufacturer; record in torque log.

### Protection Coordination (per TiN settings and relay capabilities)
- Base settings on TiN STS MV switchgear protection setting sheet: [open](Appendices%20copy/11%29%20TiN%20STS%20MV%20switchgear%20protection%20setting%20value.pdf).
- Verify time-current coordination using relay curves/features; record relay model and firmware per vendor doc: [open](Appendices%20copy/12%29%20Protection%20Relay.pdf).
- Interface with employer-supplied UniGear ZS1 switchgear; maintain clearances/interlocks per panel data: [open](Appendices%20copy/13%29%20UniGear%C2%A0ZS1.pdf). Final setting sign-off by TiN.
- Execute secondary injection and logic checks; witness and record ITRs with instrument serials.

We adopt the TiN short-circuit calculation as the governing fault envelope for thermal/mechanical withstand checks (IEC 60949) and relay pickup/time-dial margining. Values will be rechecked against as-built impedances before energization: [open](Appendices%20copy/14%29%20TiN%20Short-circuit%20current%20calculation-MV%20trailing%20cable%2050mm%C2%B2.pdf).

### Transformer Data Influence
